# Pakkathu Kadai
### A Digital Platform for Connecting Local Shops and Customers

**Version:** 1.0 – Working Prototype  
**Developed by:** Harini R.  
**Project Domain:** E-Commerce | Retail Technology | Local Business Digitisation

---

## 1. Project Overview

Pakkathu Kadai is a web-based platform designed to connect neighbourhood grocery shops with customers through a unified digital shopping experience.

The platform aims to support local retailers by improving product accessibility, simplifying shopping and introducing digital tools for inventory and order management. It also explores collaborative purchasing opportunities that enable small shopkeepers to combine their wholesale requirements.

The prototype provides separate interfaces for four user roles:

- Customers
- Shopkeepers
- Delivery Partners
- Administrators

Pakkathu Kadai focuses on making local grocery businesses more accessible, organised and digitally connected.

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional neighbourhood grocery businesses often face challenges in adopting digital solutions and managing day-to-day operations.

Key challenges include:

- Limited online visibility of local shops and their products.
- Difficulty discovering and comparing products across local retailers.
- Manual inventory and order management.
- Limited access to digital business management tools.
- Individual wholesale purchasing by small retailers.
- Time-consuming stock entry and inventory updates.

Pakkathu Kadai explores a unified digital platform to address these challenges and support the digital transformation of neighbourhood businesses.

## 4. Key Innovations

### 4.1 Voice-Based Inventory Management

A voice-based inventory entry feature designed to simplify stock management for shopkeepers.

**Key purpose:**
- Enable voice-based product and stock entry.
- Reduce repetitive manual data entry.
- Make inventory updates more convenient.
- Support easier interaction with inventory tools.

### 4.2 AI-Powered Smart Inventory

An intelligent inventory support concept designed to help shopkeepers make informed stock-management decisions.

**Features:**
- Smart stock insights.
- Low-stock alerts.
- Restock suggestions.
- AI-based inventory insights.
- Discount suggestions.

### 4.3 Collaborative Bulk Procurement

A shared wholesale purchasing model that explores collaboration between neighbourhood retailers.

Instead of purchasing independently, multiple shopkeepers can combine their requirements for the same product.

**Example:**

| Shopkeeper | Required quantity |
|---|---:|
| Shop A | 10 kg |
| Shop B | 15 kg |
| Shop C | 25 kg |
| **Combined requirement** | **50 kg** |

By combining their requirements, shopkeepers can explore bulk purchasing opportunities and negotiate potentially better wholesale prices.

**Objective:** Encourage retailer collaboration and explore ways to reduce individual procurement costs.

## 5. Platform Features

### 5.1 Customer Dashboard

Provides customers with a digital shopping experience.

- Explore local grocery shops.
- Browse available products.
- Search for shops and products.
- Filter products by category.
- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Increase or decrease product quantities.
- View cart totals.
- Proceed to checkout.
- Access promotions.
- Earn and manage reward points.

### 5.2 Shopkeeper Dashboard

**Inventory Management**
- Add and manage products.
- Update product prices.
- Update available stock quantities.
- Monitor low-stock items.

- Download inventory data as CSV.

**Order Management**
- View and manage customer orders.
- Access delivery and pickup workflows.

**Business Insights**
- Customer insights.
- Customer engagement tools.
- Smart stock insights.
- Restock suggestions.
- AI-assisted inventory insights.
- Discount suggestions.
- Product price-change tracking.
- Product profit calculator.
- Sales analytics.
- Business analytics.
- Expense management.
- Business summary.

**Shared Purchasing**
- Explore collaborative wholesale purchasing opportunities.

### 5.3 Delivery Partner Dashboard

Provides a dedicated interface for delivery operations.

- View delivery-related orders.
- Access order information.
- Manage delivery progress.
- Update delivery status.

### 5.4 Admin Dashboard

Provides administrative access to platform management features.

- Monitor products.
- View and manage orders.

- Manage platform roles.
- Support platform-level administration.

## 8. System Architecture

Pakkathu Kadai follows a frontend–backend architecture.

**Frontend Layer**

HTML, CSS and JavaScript provide the website interface, shopping experience and role-based dashboards.

**Backend Layer**

Supabase provides backend services, database connectivity and authentication.

**Database Layer**

Supabase Database manages platform information such as products, orders and user-related data.

**Deployment Layer**

GitHub Pages hosts the web-based prototype, while GitHub maintains the source code repository.
