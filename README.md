# Pakkathu Kadai
### A Digital Platform for Connecting Local Shops and Customers

**Version:** 1.0 – Working Prototype  
**Developed by:** Harini R.  
**Project Domain:** E-Commerce | Local Business Digitisation

---

## 1. Project Overview

Pakkathu Kadai is a web-based platform designed to connect local grocery shops with customers through a unified digital shopping experience.

The project aims to support neighbourhood businesses by improving product accessibility, simplifying shopping and introducing collaborative purchasing opportunities for local shopkeepers.

The prototype provides separate dashboards for customers, shopkeepers, delivery partners and administrators.

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional local grocery shopping often involves challenges such as:

- Limited online visibility of local shops and their products.
- Difficulty comparing products across nearby shops.
- Manual inventory and order management.
- Limited digital integration for small retailers.
- Individual wholesale purchasing by small shopkeepers.

Pakkathu Kadai explores a digital approach to addressing these challenges.

## 3. Objectives

- Digitise neighbourhood grocery shopping.
- Connect customers with nearby local retailers.
- Simplify inventory and order management.
- Support collaboration between local shopkeepers.
- Explore opportunities for cost-effective bulk purchasing.
- Encourage the digital growth of small businesses.

## 4. Key Features

### Customer Dashboard
- Browse local grocery shops.
- Search for shops and products.
- Filter products by category.
- Add products to the shopping cart.
- Manage product quantities.
- View cart totals.
- Proceed to checkout.

### Shopkeeper Dashboard
- Manage product inventory.
- Update product prices and stock quantities.
- Access and manage customer orders.
- Explore shared wholesale purchasing.

### Delivery Partner Dashboard
- Dedicated delivery partner interface.
- Delivery-related order access.
- Delivery progress management.

### Admin Dashboard
- Dedicated administrative interface.
- Platform management features.
- Role-based access structure.

## 5. Major Innovation – Shared Wholesale Purchasing

**Collaborative Bulk Procurement**

This feature explores a purchasing model in which multiple local shopkeepers combine their requirements for the same product.

Example:

| Shopkeeper | Required quantity |
|---|---:|
| Shop A | 10 kg |
| Shop B | 15 kg |
| Shop C | 25 kg |
| **Combined requirement** | **50 kg** |

By combining their requirements, shopkeepers can explore bulk purchasing opportunities and negotiate potentially better wholesale prices.

**Objective:** Reduce individual procurement costs and encourage collaboration among neighbourhood retailers.

## 8. System Architecture

The prototype follows a frontend–backend architecture.

**Frontend:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript provide the user interface and dashboard functionality.

**Backend:** Supabase provides database services and authentication.

**Hosting:** GitHub Pages is used to deploy the website.
